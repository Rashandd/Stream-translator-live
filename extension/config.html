StreamDubber Live Captions

How it works

Captions are produced by the StreamDubber SDK running on your streaming PC and translated in the StreamDubber cloud. Viewers pick their language right on the player — they never install anything.

Requirement: a StreamDubber account with the SDK connected. Manage both from your dashboard.
